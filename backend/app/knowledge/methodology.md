# Methodology

## Where the data comes from
Public works are discovered from OpenStreetMap, where volunteers tag roads, rail lines, bridges and public buildings as under construction. This is the only openly licensed, continuously updated, India-wide source of "work in progress". It is refreshed every six hours. OpenStreetMap does not contain sanctioned amounts or contractor names, so those appear only when an official document (tender, work order, completion certificate, RTI reply) has been attached to a project and checked by a moderator.

## What "verified" means
A citizen observation is called Verified only after a human moderator has checked that (1) the photo has a location and a capture time, or a location the citizen confirmed, (2) the location is within a reasonable distance of the project, (3) the photo is not a duplicate, and (4) personal information has been removed. Verified means the observation is credible. It does not mean the work is good or bad, and it is not a finding about any person or organisation.

## What "under review", "disputed" and "insufficient evidence" mean
Under review: submitted and waiting for a moderator. Disputed: a moderator or the responsible organisation has raised a question, and both sides remain visible. Insufficient evidence: there is not enough reviewed evidence to say anything either way.

## Why a project shows as delayed
A project is shown as "appears delayed" only when a published expected date (for example the OpenStreetMap opening_date tag or a date in an attached work order) has passed and no completion has been recorded. It does not say why, and it does not say anyone is responsible.

## Completed on OpenStreetMap
When a work is no longer tagged as under construction on OpenStreetMap, Kaam Dikhao shows it as completed, marked as a community-mapping signal. It is not an official completion record until a document is attached.

## Satellite evidence
Kaam Dikhao reads Sentinel-2 imagery from the Copernicus programme at 10 metres per pixel. That is enough to see a new road corridor, cleared land or large surface changes. It is not enough to judge the quality of a surface, a pothole or a drain cover. The change indicator compares cloud-free scenes about a year apart using vegetation (NDVI) and brightness change inside a 1.2 km window. It is a candidate observation for human review and never evidence of wrongdoing.

## Project health score
The score is a weighted mean of four components: timeline (30 percent, needs a published expected date), public records linked (20 percent), verified ground evidence (30 percent), and share of verified observations that report no problem (20 percent). Components without data are left out and shown as unavailable. If less than 40 percent of the weight is measurable, no score is shown. It is a composite of public evidence and not a legal or audit finding.

## What AI does and does not do
AI drafts and suggests: it reads text from PDFs and scans, transcribes voice notes, proposes a category and neutral wording for a photo, proposes matching projects, summarises RTI replies and drafts RTI applications. AI never publishes anything, never decides responsibility and never accuses. Every AI output is labelled as a candidate and passes a human moderator.

## Moderation
Nothing submitted by a citizen is public until a moderator approves it. Each decision is recorded in an audit log with the reason. Accusatory language is rewritten into neutral observation, and phone numbers, email addresses, ID numbers and vehicle plates are removed automatically. Faces in photos are blurred automatically. Moderators check every photo before it is published.

## Right of reply and corrections
Any department, agency or contractor can respond to any project or observation. The response is shown next to the original evidence and nothing is silently removed. Anyone can request a correction or removal through the grievance page. Complaints are acknowledged immediately and resolved within 15 days.

## Contractor and ward views
Report cards show counts (projects, statuses, evidence coverage). They do not rank or rate anyone. Contractors appear only when an attached official record names them.
