# Your rights and how to use them

## Right to Information (RTI Act 2005)
Any Indian citizen can ask a public authority for records by writing to its Public Information Officer (PIO). The reply is due within 30 days (48 hours if a person's life or liberty is involved). If there is no reply, or it is unsatisfactory, you can file a first appeal within 30 days and a second appeal to the Central or State Information Commission within 90 days. Central bodies accept online applications at rtionline.gov.in with a fee of Rs 10. States and municipal corporations have their own fee and mode.

## What to ask in an RTI about a public work
Sanction order and amount, tender notice and award, work order with dates, extension of time, measurement book and completion certificate, payments made, quality inspection reports and the defect liability period. Kaam Dikhao drafts these questions neutrally. You review and file it yourself.

## Other channels
CPGRAMS (pgportal.gov.in) takes grievances about central government bodies. Cities have their own complaint portals, listed on each project page. The Central Public Procurement Portal (eprocure.gov.in) and state e-tender portals publish tenders. Data.gov.in publishes open government datasets.

## Your data
Kaam Dikhao removes location details from the photo file, blurs faces and never publishes your name or contact details unless you choose to. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask to see, correct or delete the data you gave us.
